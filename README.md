## Welcome to GDG OAU

Google Developer Group On Campus, OAU. We are student developers at Obafemi Awolowo University who learn, build and ship together. You do not need to be an expert to join. You need curiosity and the habit of finishing what you start.

## What We Do

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="25%"><h3>Learn</h3>Hands-on sessions and study tracks led by students and invited speakers.</td>
    <td width="25%"><h3>Build</h3>Real projects with real code review, shipped in the open.</td>
    <td width="25%"><h3>Share</h3>Videos, write-ups and talks that explain how things work.</td>
    <td width="25%"><h3>Connect</h3>Peers, mentors and alumni across the Google developer ecosystem.</td>

## Our Project

### Secure Screen Lab

[![Kotlin](https://img.shields.io/badge/Kotlin-1A73E8?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/GDGOAU-MOBILE/secure-screen-lab)
[![Android](https://img.shields.io/badge/Android-188038?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/GDGOAU-MOBILE/secure-screen-lab)
[![Mobile track](https://img.shields.io/badge/Mobile_track-F9AB00?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/GDGOAU-MOBILE)

An Android app that shows how the platform protects sensitive content from screenshots, screen recording and unauthorized capture. Five hands-on experiments cover `FLAG_SECURE`, capture detection and how DRM differs from a window flag.

[View the repository](https://github.com/GDGOAU-MOBILE/secure-screen-lab)

## Get Involved

1. Visit [gdgoau.com](https://www.gdgoau.com) and say hello.
2. Pick a track and commit to it for the session.
3. Find an issue labeled `good first issue` in any repository.
4. Open a pull request with a small, focused change.
5. Present what you built.

## Code of Conduct

Be kind, give feedback on the work and never on the person, credit other people, and test security techniques only on your own devices and apps or with permission.
